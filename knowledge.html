Not everything people say becomes AI truth.

AfriVerse separates what has been contributed from what has been reviewed and validated. The goal is not to remove community knowledge, but to make its provenance and confidence visible.

01

Community reported

An original contribution from a member of the AfriVerse community.

02

Community reviewed

Other contributors review, challenge or corroborate the original contribution.

03

Expert reviewed

Relevant domain experts provide additional validation where expertise is required.

Validation is part of the knowledge architecture. A contribution can remain valuable even when it has not yet reached the highest validation level. AfriVerse should preserve the distinction between community experience, reviewed knowledge and expert validation rather than presenting all information with the same level of certainty.
