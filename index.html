The knowledge layer for Africa-native AI.

AfriVerse is a participatory knowledge platform where Africans can contribute, validate, discover and structure local knowledge, languages and lived experience for the next generation of intelligent applications.

✦ Ask AfriVerseContribute knowledge →Explore the thesis

AI can reason.
It still needs context.

Africa has enormous practical, cultural and linguistic knowledge. The opportunity is to make this knowledge structured, discoverable, trustworthy and useful to AI.

01

Fragmented

Knowledge lives across communities, languages, institutions and generations.

02

Underrepresented

Local languages, lived experience and African constraints remain unevenly represented.

03

Disconnected

The people who possess contextual knowledge are rarely connected to systems capable of structuring it.

One platform.
Multiple surfaces.

The public website explains the mission. The AfriVerse app is where people ask, contribute, discover and build the network.
