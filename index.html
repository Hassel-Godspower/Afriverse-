AfriVerse was conceived around a simple observation: Africa does not lack knowledge. It lacks enough infrastructure for that knowledge to be systematically captured, connected and made useful to modern AI.

The vision is to build a technology platform that allows African communities to participate directly in the creation of the knowledge systems that will influence the future of AI on the continent.
